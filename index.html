Classic Pancakes

Fluffy, golden pancakes that are quick to make and perfect for a weekend breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar and baking powder in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk the milk, egg and melted butter in another bowl.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients until just mixed.
  4. Pour 1/4 cup of batter onto a hot greased pan.
  5. Cook until bubbles form, flip, and cook until golden.
  6. Serve warm with syrup or fruit.
